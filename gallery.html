Urbiztondo Beach

The surfing heart of the North. It is perfect for both beginners and seasoned surfers, especially during the swell season from October to March.

Immuki Island

Known for its crystal-clear lagoons and mangrove formations, it’s a great spot for snorkeling and relaxing in natural saltwater pools.

Baluarte Watch Tower

A sentinel of the sea for over 400 years, the Baluarte Watchtower in Luna is where history meets the tides.

Agoo Eco-Park (Agoo)

Famous for its picturesque "tunnel" of tall pine trees, this park is a popular spot for photography, biking, and picnics.

Seven Hills (San Fernando)

A cluster of hills that provides a challenging but rewarding hike for outdoor enthusiasts.

San Juan Night Market

The evening food park and market scene along the main road of San Juan is a must-visit for local street food.
