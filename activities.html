Activities in La Union

From thrilling adventures to relaxing coastal experiences, La Union offers activities for every type of traveler.

🏄 Surfing Experience

La Union is the Surfing Capital of the North. You can find world-class waves in San Juan, specifically at Urbiztondo Beach. Local instructors are available for hourly lessons, providing a board and guidance for beginners to catch their first wave.

🧗 Cliff Jumping & River Trekking

Head to Tangadan Falls in San Gabriel for an adrenaline rush. The activity involves a scenic trek through rivers and forest trails, ending at a majestic waterfall where you can jump from cliffs ranging from 10 to 40 feet into deep natural pools.

🚶 Heritage & Cultural Walk

Explore the history of La Union by visiting the Baluarte Watchtower in Luna and the Ma-Cho Temple in San Fernando. These sites tell the story of the province's colonial past and its diverse cultural influences over the centuries.

🌾 Agri-Tourism & Farm Tours

Visit the famous Grape Farms in Bauang where you can experience "Pick and Pay" harvesting. You can also explore local bee farms and dragon fruit plantations to see how La Union's fertile land supports sustainable agriculture.

🎨 Pottery & Local Arts

Visit the Red Clay Pagdamilian in San Juan to learn the traditional art of pottery. You can try your hand at the potter's wheel and create your own ceramic souvenirs, or visit local galleries showcasing the works of La Union's vibrant artist community.

🌅 Sunset & Beach Relaxation

There is nothing like a La Union Sunset. The coastline offers a front-row seat to one of the most beautiful golden hours in the Philippines. It's the perfect time to unwind at a beachfront cafe or simply sit on the sand and enjoy the view.
