Activities in La Union

From thrilling adventures to relaxing coastal experiences, La Union offers activities for every type of traveler. Explore our curated list of experiences below.

🏄 Surfing Experience

La Union is recognized as the Surfing Capital of the North. Urbiztondo Beach in San Juan is the primary hub, offering consistent swells suitable for all levels.

  • Beginners: Calm "beach breaks" are perfect for first-timers. Lessons usually cost around ₱400-₱500 per hour, including board rental and a personal instructor.
  • Advanced: Visit during the peak season (October to March) for larger, more challenging waves.
  • Pro Tip: Early morning sessions (6:00 AM) offer the calmest winds and the best water clarity.

🧗 Cliff Jumping & River Trekking

Escape the beach for a day and head to the mountains of San Gabriel to visit the majestic Tangadan Falls.

  • The Journey: Enjoy a 20-minute scenic trek across rivers and forest paths or a thrilling 4x4 ride to reach the falls.
  • The Thrill: Jump from various tiers ranging from 10 to 40 feet into a deep, refreshing natural basin. Life vests are provided for safety.
  • Bamboo Rafting: You can also rent a bamboo raft to get directly under the heavy cascade for a natural water massage.

🚶 Heritage & Cultural Walk

La Union boasts a rich blend of Spanish colonial history and indigenous culture. A walk through its historical sites is a journey back in time.

  • Baluarte Watchtower: Located in Luna, this 400-year-old restored Spanish fortress stood as a sentinel against pirate attacks.
  • Ma-Cho Temple: A towering Taoist temple in San Fernando dedicated to the sea goddess Mazu, offering stunning views of the bay.
  • Namacpacan Church: One of the oldest churches in the region, known for its massive pillars and resilient architecture.

🌾 Agri-Tourism & Farm Tours

Experience the "Pick and Pay" culture in La Union’s thriving agricultural sector. It's a great activity for families and groups.

  • Grape Picking: Visit Bauang's famous vineyards. The best time to visit is during the harvest seasons (March-May).
  • Immuki Island: Explore mangrove-rich areas and learn about the local marine ecosystem and traditional fishing.
  • Local Produce: Beyond grapes, explore honeybee farms and dragon fruit plantations that showcase the province's diverse harvest.

🎨 Pottery & Local Arts

San Juan is not just for surfers; it's a haven for creatives. Immerse yourself in the local art scene.

  • Red Clay Pagdamilian: Learn the ancient art of pottery. For a small fee, local masters will teach you how to mold clay on a traditional manual wheel.
  • Luna Pebble Beach: Visit the stone art displays along the shore, where locals create intricate balance sculptures and pebble mosaics.
  • Art Galleries: Check out pop-up galleries near the surf shops that feature coastal-inspired paintings and sustainable crafts.

🌅 Sunset & Beach Relaxation

La Union is famous for its "Golden Hour." After a long day of adventure, there is no better way to recharge.

  • Best Spots: Urbiztondo Beach and San Fernando Bay offer unobstructed views of the sun dipping into the West Philippine Sea.
  • Beachfront Dining: Enjoy fresh seafood or local "Ilocano" dishes at the many restaurants that line the coast.
  • Beach Bonfires: Many hostels organize acoustic nights and small bonfires under the stars, creating a cozy community vibe.
