🏄 Surfing Experience

La Union is known as the surfing capital of Northern Philippines. Beginners can take lessons from local instructors, while experienced surfers can enjoy consistent waves along the coast.

🧗 Cliff Jumping & River Adventure

Explore natural rivers and waterfalls while experiencing cliff jumping and river trekking. Perfect for thrill seekers looking for an adrenaline rush.

⛪ Heritage & Cultural Tours

Walk through historical landmarks, churches, and heritage sites that showcase the rich culture and history of La Union.

🚜 Agri-Tourism

Visit farms, vineyards, and local plantations. Experience fruit picking and learn about agricultural practices in the province.

🎨 Arts & Crafts

Discover local craftsmanship including pottery making and handmade products. Support local artists and bring home unique souvenirs.

🌅 Sunset & Beach Relaxation

Enjoy breathtaking sunsets along the coastline. Perfect for relaxing, photography, and unwinding after a day of adventure.
