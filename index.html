Popular Destinations

Luna, La Union
Luna, officially the Municipality of Luna, is a coastal municipality in the province of La Union, Philippines, known for its pristine pebble beaches and historical sites.
San Juan, La Union
San Juan, officially the Municipality of San Juan, is a coastal municipality in the province of La Union, Philippines. According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 41,319 people. It is known as the Surfing Capital of the North.
Thunderbird Resorts (San Fernando)
Often called the "Santorini of Asia," this luxury resort is famous for its blue-and-white architecture and clifftop views.
Tangadan Falls (San Gabriel)
A majestic waterfall accessible via a trek or a scenic jeepney ride. It features a natural pool perfect for a cold dip and cliff jumping.
